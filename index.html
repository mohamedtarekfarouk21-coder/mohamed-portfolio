About

Automation that keeps working after the demo.

I design workflows that take repetitive, error-prone work off people's plates, so teams spend their time on decisions instead of data entry.

With n8n, AI agents, APIs and webhooks, I connect the tools a team already uses into one flow: data comes in, AI processes or scores it, it lands in the right place, and the right person gets notified.

My work covers the full chain: reading documents with LlamaParse, scoring them with LLM chains and structured output, answering questions from a Supabase knowledge base (RAG), and running conversational agents with memory on Telegram and chat. Everything connects to Gmail, Google Sheets and Drive, so the result lands where the team already works.

Before building anything, I map how the process runs today and where time is lost. Then I design a solution that is simple, stable and easy for non-technical people to use, test it with real data, and hand it over so it keeps working without me.

I'm currently training in AI and workflow automation at DEPI while studying Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence at Beni-Suef University, and I use Python and Docker alongside n8n when a workflow needs more than low-code.

HR Helper: AI CV screening

Receives CVs through a form, scores them with AI and logs the result in a sheet. An HR chat agent then answers questions about candidates and emails an acceptance on request.

The problemReading and ranking CVs by hand is slow, and answers about candidates end up scattered across files and inboxes.
The solutionEvery CV is parsed, scored and logged automatically, and one chat assistant answers questions and sends acceptance emails.

The solutionRows are sorted, filtered and answered automatically the moment they are added to the sheet.
